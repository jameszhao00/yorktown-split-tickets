    Town of Yorktown · Nov 3, 2026 general election · unofficial

    Ed Lachterman (R) won supervisor by 5.6 points. The federal and statewide races were much closer inside Yorktown: Mike Lawler (R) carried the town by 1.1 in the House race and Bruce Blakeman (R) by 0.9 for governor, while Westchester as a whole went 66–34 for Hochul and 63–37 for Conley.

    How to read this

    • Margin is two-party: Democratic votes minus Republican votes, as a share of the two combined. Write-ins are left out. Each candidate's votes are summed across every line they appeared on (Lachterman: Republican and Conservative; Mirchandani: Democratic and Community First; Lawler: Republican and Conservative; Conley: Democratic and Working Families).
    • Split marks districts where the supervisor winner and the comparison-race winner came from different parties.
    • Gap is how many points more Republican the supervisor race was than the comparison race in the same district. Amber means Lachterman ran ahead of his party's candidate; teal means Mirchandani ran ahead of hers.
    • Net crossover voters is the gap in Republican share times the district's two-party vote. It is a floor, not a count: voters who split in opposite directions cancel out, and some people skip the supervisor race or the other one.
    • Taconic distances are measured from the middle of each district to the nearest point on the parkway.
    • Landmarks and restaurants come from OpenStreetMap, which covers schools and parks well but lists only some of the town's restaurants.
    • Trip stops go to a landmark inside each district (a park, shop, school or town building), or the district's middle where it has none. Your stops are kept in this browser only.
    • Your location is read by your own browser only after you tap “Start driving”. The page never sends or saves it. While it's on, the page keeps your screen awake.
    • Results are unofficial. They include early, Election Day and absentee ballots; affidavit ballots had not been added yet, so totals can still move a little.
