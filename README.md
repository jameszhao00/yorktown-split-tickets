# Yorktown Split Tickets

A drive-around map of ticket splitting in the Town of Yorktown, NY, in the Nov 3, 2026 general election (unofficial results).

Open https://jameszhao00.github.io/yorktown-split-tickets/ on your phone and tap **Start driving**: the map follows your location, and the card at the bottom shows the district you're in and how it voted. **Trip** builds a list of stops (suggested ones, or any district you tap) and opens it as driving directions in Google Maps. **Info** has the district guide, the full table and notes.

Sources: Westchester County Board of Elections results; Westchester County GIS (districts, reservoirs and lakes); street map tiles © Esri, HERE, Garmin, © OpenStreetMap contributors; landmarks © OpenStreetMap contributors.
